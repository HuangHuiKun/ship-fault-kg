Title:
Anonymised vibration, lubricant and availability dataset for multimodal condition-based maintenance of tugboat propulsion systems

Creators:
Miguel Herrera Suárez; Rafael Andrés Marcillo; Roberto Manuel Torres; Julio Nolberto Pérez Guerrero

Affiliation:
Universidad Técnica de Manabí

Description:
This dataset supports a Reliability Engineering & System Safety manuscript on vibration-led multimodal condition-based maintenance decision support for tugboat propulsion. It contains anonymised processed tables, representative FFT spectral features, Spectral Severity Score (SSS), Oil Degradation Index (ODI), Lead-Time Index (LTI), diagnostic-zone economic data, final figures, and Python scripts for summary checks and plotting. Original vessel names, operator identity, port schedules and commercially sensitive records are withheld under confidentiality agreements; engine-family case labels are used for traceability.

Keywords:
condition-based maintenance; vibration spectroscopy; tugboat propulsion; Lead-Time Index; multimodal diagnosis; Spectral Severity Score; decision support
