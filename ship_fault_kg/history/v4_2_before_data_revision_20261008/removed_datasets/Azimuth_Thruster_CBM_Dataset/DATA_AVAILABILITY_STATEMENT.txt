The anonymised supporting dataset has been prepared for Zenodo under reserved DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20101612. The public DOI will resolve when the Zenodo record is published; a private reviewer link and the complete dataset archive can be supplied through Editorial Manager if the record remains in draft during peer review. The package includes processed severity scores, representative FFT spectral features, lubricant-analysis variables, Python code, final figures, diagnostic-zone economic tables, regression-diagnostic notes and the anonymised aggregate SSS-H_t table. Raw vessel-identifying files, point-level FFT arrays tied to proprietary route files, port identities, operator name and contract-level economic data are withheld under confidentiality and data-ownership restrictions. Data are released under CC BY 4.0 and code under the MIT license.
